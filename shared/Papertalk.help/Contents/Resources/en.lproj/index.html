Welcome to Papertalk

Turn a scientific paper into a podcast episode, right on your Mac. An interviewer talks with one of the paper's authors, in neural voices, and a mind map lays out the key ideas.

Make your first episode

How it works

  1. Import a PDFDrop it on the library or press ⌘O, then pick how deep the episode goes.
  2. Choose the voicesPapertalk proposes the cast. Rename them, swap voices, hear a sample.
  3. ListenPlay it in the app, from Control Center or with your headphones' buttons.
