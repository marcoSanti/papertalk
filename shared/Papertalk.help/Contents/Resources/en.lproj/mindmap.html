Papertalk Help

Mind maps

Every paper gets a mind map: its key concepts around three branches, the problem, the approach and the demonstration.

Read a map

Open it with Mind map in the paper's details. It opens on a plain-language summary. Click a concept to see its core idea and description, or a connection to see the two concepts it joins.

Move around

  • Zoom with the controls or by pinching on the trackpad; Fit the whole map shows everything.
  • Drag a concept to move it, or a connection's dot or label to bend it.
  • Reset (the arrow by the zoom controls) puts everything back. The arrangement lasts while the window is open.

Edit

Click Edit by the zoom controls to rename, describe, add, move and delete concepts, and to add, name and delete connections. Changes are saved as you make them. The three main concepts can be changed but not deleted.

Connections to other papers

When a map is drawn, Papertalk looks for concepts it shares with your other papers' maps and shows them as grey, dashed nodes around the edge. Click one to see the other paper, and Open its mind map to switch to it. A connection shows on both papers' maps; deleting it on one removes it from the other.

Redraw

Redraw mind map draws one paper's map again. File → Redraw All Mind Maps… redraws every map, one after another, after asking. Edits to the current maps are lost.
