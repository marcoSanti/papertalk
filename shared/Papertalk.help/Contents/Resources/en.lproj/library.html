Papertalk Help

Find your papers

Grid or list

Use the Layout button in the toolbar to switch between covers in a grid and a list, and Sort to change the order. Use the arrow keys to move around the grid.

Search

Type in the search field to search titles, authors and DOIs. Start with # to search tags, for example #neuroscience.

Sidebar

  • All Papers and Favorites. Favourite a paper with the star in its details.
  • Libraries: folders for your papers (see Libraries).
  • Tags: the most-used tags first. Click one to see its papers.
  • Authors: everyone in your library, like a contact book, with their papers and co-authors.

Details

Select a paper to see it on the right: play it, read the transcript, open the PDF or the mind map, ask about it, favourite it, or fix its metadata. The button in the toolbar hides or shows the details pane, and you can drag its edge to resize it.

Details (under those buttons), a double-click on the paper, or Open in its context menu opens the paper in a window of its own, with its authors, details, tags, activity log and every action. See Work with a paper.

Tags and metadata

Title, authors, affiliations, DOI and tags are read from the paper. In the paper's window, add a tag (suggested tags are offered), or click the × on one to remove it. Edit metadata… changes the rest, and Refresh metadata reads title, authors and DOI again. With a DOI, Papertalk fetches a BibTeX citation: Show citation and Copy citation.

Under Details, Cites shows how many papers this one cites; click it to list them. The list comes from Crossref when the DOI has one there, otherwise it's read from the PDF's References section (this needs the LLM and can miss or garble an entry). For each cited paper, the arrow opens its DOI, or a Google Scholar search when it has none, and the copy button copies it as one line of text. In library marks a paper you already have (same DOI or title): click it to go to that paper. Refresh metadata tries again for a paper whose list is still missing.
