Papertalk Help

Suggested papers

Papertalk can find papers like the ones in your library, rank them by how well they fit, and import the ones you pick.

Turn it on

Click Suggested Papers in the sidebar (or choose File → Show Suggested Papers) and switch on Check every day in the bar at the top. It's off until you turn it on, because a check sends the DOIs of the papers in your library to Semantic Scholar. Nothing else about your papers is sent.

Once a day, while Papertalk is open, it asks Semantic Scholar for papers like up to 100 of your most recent ones. When new papers turn up, a notification says how many (a message at the bottom of the window if Papertalk is in front), and the sidebar row counts them. Click the notification to open the list. Check Now checks right away, even with the daily check off. The section's subtitle says when it last checked.

Suggestions start from DOIs, so papers without one aren't used. Add a missing DOI with Edit paper. Papers already in your library, and papers you dismissed or imported, are never suggested again.

Read and pick

Each suggested paper is a row with its title, authors, year and source. If an LLM is set in Settings → LLM Settings, it ranks the list by how well each paper fits your library and adds a line on why, marked with sparkles. Without one, the list keeps Semantic Scholar's order.

Click a row to see the paper in the details panel on the right: every author, why it fits, its abstract and DOI. From there, Import it, Open in Browser to read more, or Dismiss it.

Each row says how its PDF arrives:

  • Free PDF: Papertalk downloads it itself.
  • Download in browser: you download it, as below.

Each row also has its own Import button, and a Dismiss button that hides the paper for good; right-click a row for the same actions. Imports use the depth, length and language of your last import from the import sheet, shown in the top bar as Imported as. Import more papers while one is on its way and they wait their turn, marked Queued.

Get a paper through your browser

Papers are fetched one at a time, in a card above the list. When a paper has no free PDF, or its free link doesn't give a PDF, the card shows these steps:

  1. Click Open in Browser to open the paper's page.
  2. Download the PDF. Sign in through your library or institution if the page asks.
  3. Papertalk picks up the new PDF from your Downloads folder and imports it.

While it waits, the card shows Waiting for a new PDF in Downloads…. Only a PDF that arrives after the wait begins counts, and only once the browser has finished saving it. Skip leaves the paper in the list and moves to the next; Stop ends the imports, and the papers not imported stay in the list.

If the PDF doesn't look like the suggested paper (its title isn't on the first pages), it's imported anyway and a message says so. Delete it if it's the wrong paper.
