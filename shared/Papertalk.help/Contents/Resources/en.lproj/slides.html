Papertalk Help

Slide decks

Papertalk can turn a presentation into an episode, like a paper. Export the deck as a PDF first (Keynote, PowerPoint and Google Slides all can).

Import a deck

  • Open the import sheet and turn on This is a slide deck. Opened from Slides in the sidebar, it starts on.
  • Pick the depth, length and language as for a paper (see Import a paper).

Slides are often mostly pictures. For each slide with little text, Papertalk shows the slide to your LLM as an image and asks what it shows: charts, diagrams, tables and photos. The descriptions are saved with the deck, so a rerun, a mind map or Ask doesn't describe the slides again.

This needs a model that reads images. If the model turns the first image down, the rest of the deck is read from its text only, and the paper's log says so.

The Slides library

Decks are kept in Slides in the sidebar, apart from your papers: they don't show in All Papers, Favorites, Authors, the tags or your libraries, and a deck with the same title as a paper isn't treated as a duplicate.

Prompts

Two prompts in Settings → Query Prompts, under Slides, shape decks: Describe a slide (what the LLM is asked about each picture slide) and Slide deck note (put before the slides' text in every step, so prompts written for papers read it as a presentation).
