Libraries and paper connections

Libraries

A library is a folder for your papers, with a name and an icon, listed in the sidebar above the tags.

  • Click + beside Libraries to make one.
  • Right-click a paper and choose Move to Library to file it.
  • Right-click a library for Edit Library… or Delete Library…. Deleting a library keeps its papers, in no library.

Mind map connections stay within a library, unless you turn on connecting to other libraries when you make or edit it; such a library shows a chain icon.

Paper connections

Show Paper Connections (right-click a library, or the button beside its name in the toolbar) draws its papers as one graph. A line joins two papers whose mind maps connect, thicker for more connections, and each cluster of linked papers has its own colour.

  • Drag a paper to move it, or the background to pan. Zoom with the controls or by pinching.
  • Click a paper or a line to see which concepts connect, and open a mind map from there.
  • Papers in other libraries it connects to show in grey.
