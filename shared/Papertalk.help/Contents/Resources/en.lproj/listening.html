Papertalk Help

Listen to an episode

Play

Select a paper and press Space or Return, or click play in its details. The player bar at the bottom shows the episode, the speaker and a scrubber you can drag.

Control Center and media keys

The episode shows in Control Center's Now Playing, with the paper's cover. Play/pause, next and previous on your keyboard, AirPods or headphones work there too: next and previous jump one line of the conversation; skip forward and back move 15 seconds.

Picking up where you stopped

Every episode remembers where you stopped. Mark as listened (or Mark as unlistened) in the paper's context menu or window keeps track of what you've heard.

Read along

Click Transcript to read the script. Click a line to jump there. Copy transcript copies the whole script.

Audio after listening

When you listen to an episode to the end, Papertalk offers to delete its audio to free space; the script is kept. Keep, and don't ask again turns this off, and Settings → Clear Data turns it back on.

A paper without saved audio still plays: its voices are made live while you listen, and that audio isn't saved.
