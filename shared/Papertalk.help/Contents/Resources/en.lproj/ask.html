Papertalk Help

Ask a paper

Ask your LLM anything about a paper, and get answers drawn from the paper itself.

Open a chat

  • Select a paper and click Ask AI, beside Details in its panel.
  • Or right-click the paper and choose Ask….

The chat opens in its own window. An empty chat suggests starter questions, such as What is the main contribution? or What are the limitations?: click one to send it.

Ask

Type in Ask about this paper… and press Return to send. ⌥Return starts a new line. While the LLM works, the chat shows Reading the paper….

Every question is sent with the paper's text (read from its PDF), its details (title, authors, affiliations, published date, DOI, topics) and the chat so far, so you can ask follow-ups.

Start over

A chat lasts while Papertalk is open. New chat clears it and starts over.

Good to know

  • Answers come from the LLM set in Settings → LLM Settings. With a local LLM, the paper never leaves your Mac.
  • Scanned PDFs without a text layer can't be asked about.
  • Very long papers are cut at about 30,000 tokens, so questions about their last pages may go unanswered.
  • Change how the LLM answers by editing the Ask prompt in Settings → Query Prompts.
